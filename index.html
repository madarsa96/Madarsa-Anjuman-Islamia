About Our Institution

Where Deen Meets Modern Education

Madarsa Anjuman Islamia, located in Baraipatti, Deoria, Uttar Pradesh, is a beacon of holistic education. We believe in nurturing every child's mind, soul, and character — combining the timeless wisdom of Islamic learning with the practical tools of modern academics.
