# Madarsa Anjuman Islamia

A village school in Deoria, Uttar Pradesh, providing quality education from Nursery to Class 5, English medium, following the NCERT pattern.

## About

Madarsa Anjuman Islamia was founded with the goal of making good education accessible to children in the village — regardless of their family's financial background. We aim to build strong manners, communication skills, and logical ability in our students, helping them overcome hesitation and grow into confident speakers and listeners.

## What We Offer

- Nursery to Class 5, English medium (NCERT curriculum)
- Urdu / Arabic language instruction
- General Knowledge & General Studies (GK-GS)
- Introduction to computers
- Sports

## Affordable, No Hidden Costs

- Free uniforms and books for all students
- Minimal monthly fee of ₹200
- No additional charges — no exam fee, registration fee, sports fee, or other hidden costs

## Funding

The school runs on donations from the public. Every contribution directly supports free books, uniforms, and keeping fees low for village children.
